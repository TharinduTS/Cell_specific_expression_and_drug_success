# Cell_specific_expression_and_drug_success
 This section contains the different methods I used to study the effect of cell specific gene expression on a tested drug reaching the marketed level, taking all the other factors (like tissue specific expression, treated disease, compound etc) into account.  This includes different methods I used to compare cell type specific expression, and models I built to compare the probability of drug success.

## basic_comparison_attempt_with_plots

This R scripts shows the attempt I took to come up with a comparison method to study the affect of cell specific expression on drug success

## opentargets_data_for_comparison

This script explains methods to extract different datasets from 'opentargets' that are shown to affect drug success by different studies, and simple to complex models built to test the effect of cell type specific gene expression in drug success, even when the factors suggested by other studies are taken into consideration.
